# Skill: Content Creation

## Capability
Generate persuasive marketing content — ad copy, social captions, video hooks, product descriptions — optimized for African markets and platform constraints.

## Hook Patterns (Scroll-Stopping Techniques)
- **Pattern Break**: Unexpected opening that disrupts scrolling behavior
- **Curiosity Gap**: Open loop that demands resolution
- **Social Proof**: Lead with results or testimonials
- **Contrarian**: Challenge conventional wisdom
- **Story Hook**: Begin mid-narrative for immediate engagement

## AIDA Framework
Every piece of content should implicitly or explicitly follow:
1. **Attention**: Grab with hook (first 3 seconds / first line)
2. **Interest**: Build relevance to audience pain/desire
3. **Desire**: Paint the transformation or outcome
4. **Action**: Clear, specific call-to-action

## Platform-Specific Formats
- **Instagram**: 125 caption chars above fold, 3-5 hashtags optimal, carousel first-slide hook
- **TikTok**: First 1-2 seconds critical, text-on-screen required (sound-off consumption), CTA in last 3s
- **Facebook**: Longer copy works (up to 400 words for storytelling ads), link preview optimization
- **X/Twitter**: 280 chars, thread structure for long-form, quote-tweet mechanics
- **LinkedIn**: Professional tone, data-led hooks, 1300 char optimal for posts
- **YouTube**: Title 60 chars max, description first 2 lines visible, timestamp chapters

## Bilingual Content Rules
- Primary language = market dominant language
- Secondary language = English (pan-African lingua franca) or local second language
- Both versions must convey same message and cultural tone
- Avoid literal translation — transcreate for cultural fit
- Use local idioms where they enhance connection (not for novelty)

## Output Requirements
When this skill is active, your output MUST include:
- primary_language_content: headline + body + CTA + hashtags + hook_type_used
- secondary_language_content: headline + body + CTA + hashtags
- variants: at least 2-3 for ad_copy or video_hook output types
- cultural_elements_incorporated: summary of localization integration
