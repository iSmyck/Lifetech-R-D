# Skill: Cultural Analysis

## Capability
Analyze cultural codes, symbolism, generational lenses, and societal norms for marketing content across African markets.

## Markets Covered
- Nigeria (Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa cultural corridors)
- Kenya-Ghana (East-West African youth culture)
- South Africa (Rainbow Nation, multilingual dynamics)
- CIV-Rwanda-Botswana (Francophone + emerging markets)

## Knowledge

### Cultural Codes Framework
When analyzing a market, always evaluate:
1. **Power Distance** — hierarchy in communication
2. **Individualism vs Collectivism** — community messaging
3. **Uncertainty Avoidance** — risk in creative
4. **Long-term Orientation** — aspirational vs immediate messaging
5. **Indulgence** — emotional vs rational appeal

### Generational Lens
- Gen Z (18-25): Digital-native, meme culture, authenticity, social justice
- Millennials (26-40): Aspirational, brand-conscious, mobile-first
- Gen X (41-55): Trust-driven, value-oriented, family-focused

### Do/Don't Examples
- DO: Use local idioms naturally (e.g., "Chale" in Ghana, "Omo" in Nigeria)
- DON'T: Use religious symbols without explicit approval
- DO: Reference local success stories and role models
- DON'T: Assume one "African" culture — each market is distinct

## Output Requirements
When this skill is active, your output MUST include:
- cultural_codes_applied: list of codes used
- generational_lens: which generation and why
- sensitivity_flags: any cultural risks identified
